# OGM forest fire statistics

- Publisher: T.C. Tarım ve Orman Bakanlığı, Orman Genel Müdürlüğü (OGM), Ormancılık İstatistikleri
  (Official Statistics Programme).
- Page: https://www.ogm.gov.tr/tr/e-kutuphane/resmi-istatistikler
- File: "Ormancılık İstatistikleri 2024.zip", table **2.11 Orman yangınları, 1988-2024**
  (Forest fires: burnt area, number of fires, causes)
  https://www.ogm.gov.tr/tr/e-kutuphane-sitesi/Istatistikler/Ormanc%C4%B1l%C4%B1k%20%C4%B0statistikleri/Ormanc%C4%B1l%C4%B1k%20%C4%B0statistikleri%202024.zip
- Downloaded: 2026-11-15. `2.11_orman_yanginlari_1988-2024.xls` is the original table;
  `ogm_forest_fires_2001_2024.csv` holds the columns used here (year, number of fires, burnt area in ha).
- 2025: not yet in the official tables (the June 2026 bulletin "Forestry Statistics, 2025" gives
  no fire figures), so the comparison covers 2001–2024.
- Scope: OGM counts fires in forest land only. Satellites also detect agricultural, pasture and
  other vegetation fires, so the two series measure different things.
