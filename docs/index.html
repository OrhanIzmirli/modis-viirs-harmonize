The Fire Almanac of Türkiye, 2001–2025

When does Türkiye burn?

25 years of MODIS and VIIRS fire detections, harmonized into one calendar.

Built on NASA FIRMS data · by the FireWatch TR team

Explore the calendar

Two NASA instruments have watched Türkiye's fires from orbit since 2000 and 2012. They do not count the same way. One sees in 1 km pixels, the other in 375 m; they pass at different hours and grade their own certainty on different scales.

This almanac puts both records on one page, so that a week of fire in 2003 can be set beside a week in 2021.

Duplicates

MODIS and VIIRS often record the same fire minutes or hours apart. Records that close in space and time are merged into one fire.

Confidence

MODIS grades each detection from 0 to 100; VIIRS says low, nominal or high. Both are mapped onto one scale, and a fire recorded by both satellites gets a higher score.

Pixel size

A MODIS pixel covers about seven times the ground of a VIIRS pixel.

04

Who is this for?

A fire manager in Muğla

A fire manager in Muğla cannot position crews everywhere. The almanac shows which weeks have historically demanded them, so teams can be placed before the season starts.

A farmer's neighbour in Diyarbakır

In Diyarbakır, more fires start in October than in any other month. Stubble fires can spread into neighbouring orchards and fields. The almanac shows when burning peaks, and section 06 covers the stubble-burning ban and what to do instead.

A researcher, anywhere

Matching MODIS and VIIRS takes time. The merged events are in the files below.

05

Download the data

The page is built from three open files. MIT License; FIRMS data courtesy of NASA.

events.geojson.gz
One point per fire event: event_id, first_seen, last_seen, sensors, satellites, n_modis, n_viirs, max_frp, sum_frp, harmonized_confidence (the Trust Score, 0–1), agreement, province.

calendar_daily.csv.gz
One row per view, region and local day: mode (harmonized, modis or viirs), region (TR or a TR-xx province code), day, events, new_events, frp_mw, det_modis, det_viirs and, for harmonized, both, modis_only, viirs_only.

detections_harmonized.csv.gz
Every MODIS and VIIRS detection in one schema (lat, lon, time_utc, sensor, satellite, frp_mw, footprint_km2, frp_mw_per_km2, day_night, confidence_raw, confidence_class, confidence_score) with its event_id.

Times are UTC in the detection file and local (Europe/Istanbul) in the calendar. Method, code and tests are on GitHub.
