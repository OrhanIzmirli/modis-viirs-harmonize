When does Türkiye burn?

25 years of MODIS and VIIRS fire detections, harmonized into one calendar.

Every fire event gets a trust score — like a credit score, but for satellite detections.

Built on NASA FIRMS data · by the FireWatch TR team

Explore the calendar

Two NASA instruments have watched Türkiye's fires from orbit for a quarter of a century. They do not count the same way: one sees in 1 km pixels, the other in 375 m; they pass at different hours and grade their own certainty on different scales.

This almanac puts both records on one page, so that a week of fire in 2003 and a week of fire in 2021 can be compared fairly.

Same fire, counted twice?

MODIS and VIIRS often see the same fire minutes or hours apart. When two records are this close in space and time, we merge them into one fire.

Different confidence scales?

One instrument rates fires from 0 to 100, the other says low, nominal or high. We put both on one scale, and a fire seen by both earns extra trust.

1 km vs 375 m pixels?

A MODIS pixel covers about seven times the area of a VIIRS pixel. We model each pixel's real footprint, so a large pixel and a small one can still match.
